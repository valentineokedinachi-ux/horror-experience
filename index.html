THE HOUSE REMEMBERS
Chapter I — The Arrival

You came
back home.

The road ended twenty minutes ago. Your phone has no signal. The house stands exactly where you remember it. Same windows. Same broken gate. Same light upstairs.

But nobody has lived here for eleven years.

Then why is someone standing behind the curtain?
01 / 08
Chapter II — The Hallway

Someone
was here.

The front door opens without resistance. Inside, the air smells of wet wood and something metallic. A family photograph hangs crooked on the wall.

You recognize everyone in it. Your mother. Your father. Your younger sister.

And someone standing behind all of them.

You don't remember that person.
02 / 08
Chapter III — The Living Room

Don't look
behind you.

The living room is covered in dust. The television is on, but the screen shows nothing except static.

A chair rocks slowly in the corner. Nobody is sitting in it.

On the floor, you find a small red ribbon. It belongs to your sister.

You buried that ribbon with her.
03 / 08
Chapter IV — The Upstairs Room

Her room
is untouched.

The door is open. The little bed is still there. The wardrobe. The drawings on the wall.

A child's handwriting covers the mirror. The letters are written backwards.

You move closer.

"You promised you wouldn't tell."
04 / 08
Chapter V — The Reflection

It moved
without you.

You stand in front of the mirror. Your reflection stares back.

You raise your right hand. It raises its left.

Then it smiles. You aren't smiling.

Something is learning how to look like you.
05 / 08
Chapter VI — The Staircase

There is
someone below.

The staircase descends into darkness. You remember being told never to enter the basement.

The door is open now. A red light flickers from somewhere beneath the house.

Then you hear your mother's voice.

"Please. Don't let it know you're here."
06 / 08
Chapter VII — The Confession

You were
never alone.

There are photographs on the table. Every photograph shows the same family. Every photograph shows the same room.

But in each one, a different person is missing.

The last photograph is dated today. You are standing in it.

And behind you is the thing you let into this house.
07 / 08
Chapter VIII — The Last Confession

It was
never your house.

You remember now. The house was never haunted. It was waiting.

And the thing in the mirror? It wasn't trying to get out.

It was waiting for you to come back in.
